# Video index

An index to the [PCGen homebrew playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLLa5A1qjBOPekqEC_R9BAZW-8q5IT-klM)
— 25 videos, about 4 hours 51 minutes, recorded around PCGen 6.05 and 6.06.

The playlist is the only place the actual *workflow* is shown: which file to open, in
what order, and what goes wrong. The official documentation never covers that. This
index exists because the playlist has no index of its own.

The **Handbook page** column links to a page covering the same ground. A dash means
the handbook does not cover that topic yet — 24 of the 25 videos have a page so far.
The index is worth having either way, since it is the only index to this playlist.

!!! warning "Recorded against 6.05/6.06"
    Handbook pages are written against current `master`. Where a video shows something
    that has since changed, the handbook gives the current behaviour and says so.

    A known example: the videos and PCGen's shipped templates load feats with PCC
    `FEAT:`. That is deprecated — see
    [about the old feat file](../start/first-change.md#about-the-old-feat-file).

## How these were used

Transcripts were pulled from the auto-generated captions and read as research. They
are kept locally and are not republished — the recordings are the author's work.

Their quality sets what they can be used for. Measured across all 25 videos, the
captions contain **7 uppercase tokens in 41,273 words**. Tag names are uppercase, so
the captions carry almost no recoverable syntax, plus a few hundred mangled terms.

So the videos decide *which tasks matter and in what order*, and *what goes wrong*.
Every tag on a handbook page is taken from PCGen's source and checked automatically
before publishing. See [credits](credits.md).
